Colors

How much to use (per 1/2 cup glue):

  • Acrylic paint: a pea-sized squeeze
  • Gel food coloring: 1–2 drops (wear gloves, it stains)
  • Mica powder: 1/4 tsp
  • Eyeshadow: a small pinch, crushed fine

Mix the color into the glue BEFORE the activator.

Skip Sharpies and other permanent inks. Washable marker ink is OK.

Tap ✏️ Edit on any color to change it.

Scents & Add-ins

Household crunch

  • Broken styrofoam or packing peanuts
  • Small sponge cubes
  • Snipped plastic straws

Chunky glitter sheds less than fine glitter.

Scent tips

Scent: a few drops of kitchen extract, or 1–2 drops of essential or fragrance oil per batch. Keep it light.

Add scent after the squish forms. Patch-test oils on a small spot of skin first.
